Release Beta 0.0.70
What's new
in Strawberry
Released
November 6, 2025
This release introduces Team Plans, making Strawberry more collaborative. It focuses on faster browsing, upfront credit estimation, redesigned chat history, and multiple account support for Apps. Performance improvements make tasks 20-30% faster.
Team Plan
Collaboration is here! Invite your team members to work together in Strawberry. With unified billing and smooth user management, it’s perfect for colleagues who want to use it for work. Find under Settings → View all plans → Create Team.
Credit Estimation Up Front
Know before you go. Agents now provide credit estimates before starting tasks, giving you full transparency and control over your usage.
20-30% Faster Task Completion
Companions are now working faster. We have optimized their browsing capabilities making task completion about 20-30% faster.
New Chat History
Finding past conversations and viewing active ones is now effortless. We have redesigned chat history with improved search and a cleaner interface to help you pick up right where you left off.
Multiple Accounts on Apps
Connect multiple accounts for the same App. Switch between your personal and work Gmail, manage multiple Notion workspaces, or juggle different Google Sheets accounts - all from the Strawberry chat. Add new accounts under Settings → Integrations.
Improvements and Bug Fixes
Address bar now includes intelligent history suggestions
Invite friends directly from the new tab page (2 invites available)
New Chrome keyboard shortcuts for power users
Real-time credit usage visible in the companion progress bar
Fixed migration crashes affecting some users during updates
Resolved initial launch crashes for new installations
Pasting text in chat now correctly preserves indents and newlines
Bookmark functionality improvements and edge case fixes
