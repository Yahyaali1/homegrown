Feature update –
October 1
What's new
in Strawberry
A clearer chat, a more useful sidebar, and a better home for your files. This update makes everyday work easier to follow, review, and pick up again.
A clearer chat
Chat is easier to follow, from readable answers and page previews to clear app activity. See what your companion is doing and when it needs you. Review and edit emails, calendar events, and other app actions before approving them. Wide tables now have more room.
Your tabs and work, together
The redesigned Activity sidebar brings tabs, chats, routines, and shortcuts together. Choose what appears and sort tabs your way. We've also improved tab dragging, pinning, resizing, and where you land after closing a tab.
Workspace is now Library
Find artifacts, projects, companion files, and shared work in one place, with better search, filters, and previews. Restore recently deleted files in bulk, or inspect conflicting edits and recover the version you want.
Work and personal, side by side
Keep multiple Strawberry accounts signed in across separate browser profiles. Connect multiple accounts for the same app, name them, and choose a default. Profiles using the same Strawberry account share companions and connected apps while keeping website logins separate.
Meetings you can pick up afterward
A dedicated Meetings page brings recordings, transcripts, and summaries together, with key takeaways, decisions, and action items. Listen alongside the transcript, retry failed transcription, and start your next recording while the previous meeting finishes processing.
Credits that are easier to understand
See your remaining credits at a glance in the browser menu. Redesigned account and team pages make balances, reset times, and spending sources clearer, with more direct ways to top up, change plans, or request a seat upgrade.
Improvements and bug fixes
Search chat content with highlighted matches, or use Cmd/Ctrl+F inside a conversation.
Drafts restore more reliably; long pastes fold into expandable chips.
Faster New Tabs and smoother scrolling through chats, tabs, and bookmarks.
Dictation inserts at your cursor without replacing existing text.
Gmail signatures follow your sending address and can be switched off before approval.
Better routine editing and run history, spreadsheet pivot tools, and transparent image generation.
More reliable file sync, chat sharing, app reconnections, and Windows updates.
