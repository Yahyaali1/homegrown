Release 0.0.94
What's new
in Strawberry
Released
March 15, 2026
Introducing Routines. Your companion can now
run things on a schedule
or
react when something happens in your apps
, like drafting a reply whenever a client emails, or briefing you every morning. This update also brings fluid tab animations, a refreshed chat history sidebar, stronger security, and a long list of fixes.
Routines
Tell your companion to do something on a schedule or whenever something happens in your apps, and it gets to work. "Summarize my inbox every morning at 9," "when I get a client email, draft a reply," "when a commit lands on main, post a summary in Slack." Works with Gmail, Outlook, GitHub and Notion. Replay any past run or trigger one on demand. If your computer is off or asleep when a routine is due, it waits and runs automatically the next time you open Strawberry.
Tab Animations
The tab bar was rebuilt with fluid animations throughout. Opening a tab, closing one, dragging tabs into groups, pinning and unpinning, every action now transitions smoothly instead of snapping. When you close a tab, the remaining tabs hold their width so the close button stays right under your cursor, perfect for quickly closing several tabs in a row.
Chat History
The chat history sidebar got a fresh look. It now shows upcoming routines and when they're scheduled to run, and active routines update live so you always know what's happening.
Security & Privacy
Tighter access controls across the app and stronger handling of content from websites you visit. We also resolved known dependency vulnerabilities identified for this release.
Improvements and Bug Fixes
Companion Knowledge: Memory and skills combined in a single "Knowledge" tab, easier to manage what your companion knows.
Animations: Widgets and notifications now animate smoothly in and out instead of popping.
Tab Groups: Hovering a collapsed group previews all tabs inside with the ability to close individual tabs.
Shortcuts: More standard keyboard shortcuts now work in Strawberry.
Billing: Plan cancellations are now always honored, prevents unexpected charges.
Stop Button: "Stop generating" reliably stops the stream. The tool execution engine was rebuilt for better reliability, fewer stuck agent sessions.
Email: Preview before sending now matches the actual sent email. Gmail login from chat and Microsoft re-approval issues fixed.
Slack: Messages no longer arrive empty, and files now appear alongside the text they belong with.
Web Compatibility: Forms and login popups work correctly when opening in new tabs.
Integrations: Icons display correctly, and expired connections can be refreshed without removing and re-adding them.
Polish: Smoother address bar, better text navigation, and various small visual fixes throughout.
