Release 0.0.83
What's new
in Strawberry
Released
February 17, 2026
Strawberry Browser is now in open beta. After months of closed development, we're opening the product to everyone.
Open Beta
Anyone can now download Strawberry Browser at strawberrybrowser.com.
New Onboarding
The onboarding has been completely rethought. Strawberry now digs deeper into who you are, your role, and how you work. With your permission, it sends an agent to look you up on the web and builds a personalized profile. You get tailored suggestions for your specific workflow within minutes.
More Reliable Agents
Long-running agents are now deeply viable. They hold context across complex tasks, recover from errors, and handle multi-step workflows across login-gated platforms without stopping to ask for help. You can hand off real work and trust that it gets done.
Rebuilt from the Ground Up
The entire browser has been rebuilt in a new framework. Faster, less battery, more companions working in parallel.
Companion Memory
Your companions now remember preferences and patterns across sessions. The more you use Strawberry, the better it understands how you work.
