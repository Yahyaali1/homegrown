Release Beta 0.0.76
What's new
in Strawberry
Released
January 14, 2026
New year, tidier workspace. This update brings Tab Groups to combat tab overload, native Passkey support (including iCloud Keychain), and Profile Colors so you separate work-you from personal-you at a glance. We've also polished up some details based on your feedback.
Tab Groups
Finally, some order. Create named, color-coded groups to organize your tabs by project, client, or "stuff I'll get to eventually." Drag tabs in or out, collapse groups when you need to focus. Right-click any tab to get started.
Profile Colors
Your tab bar, your colors. Set a different color for each profile to instantly see whether you're in work mode or personal mode. No more accidentally sending that message from the wrong account. Enable in Settings → Browser profiles.
Native Passkeys & WebAuthn
Passkeys now work the way they should on Mac. Log in with Touch ID, pull passkeys straight from iCloud Keychain, and import bookmarks and passwords from Safari in seconds.
Improvements and Bug Fixes
Smart(er) Address Bar: Faster suggestions with no flickering, plus clearer loading indicators.
Chat & Files: Transcribe audio/video files (MP3/WAV) and view/edit data tables directly in the chat.
Pinned tabs: Fixed duplicating and restoration issues.
Notifications: Slack and Gmail tabs now show accurate counts.
Zoom levels: They now persist across sessions and tabs restore correctly after updates.
Duplicate chat: You can now duplicate chat sessions to try different prompting approaches without losing your original context. Open the chat history modal, right click on a chat and then click duplicate.
Window Restoration: Cmd+Shift+T now restores tabs to their original window, not just whichever one you're looking at.
Smarter Passwords: Autofill has been upgraded to handle multi-step logins (username first, then password) and works correctly within popup windows.
