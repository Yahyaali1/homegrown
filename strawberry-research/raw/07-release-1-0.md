# Strawberry 1.0 release page (https://strawberrybrowser.com/updates/strawberry-1-0)

Fetched 2026-10-29 as raw HTML stripped to text. Quotes are verbatim.

## New in 1.0 (2026-10-26)
- **Companion looks:** "Pick a color, try faces until one feels right, or hit randomize and see who shows up. It is the same companion you work with every day, now with a face you chose."
- **Hire one of nine:** "Each arrives knowing its job, with the skills and routines for it, and spends your first chat learning how you work."
- **Companion Plaza** ("Our fun mode"):
  - "Select Companion Plaza in the corner of New Tab, and your companions come out to hang around the chat input."
  - "They wander, stop to chat with each other about their day, about you, and about the sites you visited."
  - "Click a companion to start working with it, and it flies up to its spot above the chat input."
  - "Pick one up and toss it, or wiggle your pointer over a head for a headpat."
  - Image alt text: "Companions wandering around the chat input on New Tab".
  - (inferred) The companions see your browsing history, because they talk about the sites you visited.
